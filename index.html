Services

Training designed around your actual risk.

Every program is built from field experience, not a generic compliance template. We adapt each session to your industry, your facility, and the incidents your team is most likely to face.

Workplace Violence Prevention Training

Recognize warning signs early, build reporting habits that stick, and give your team a clear, rehearsed response plan before an incident happens, not after.

De-Escalation Training

Practical verbal and physical tactics for defusing hostile situations with customers, patients, tenants, or coworkers, taught through scenario-based drills, not lectures.

Healthcare Staff Safety Training

Built for clinical environments where staff are exposed to unpredictable patients and visitors. Covers restraint alternatives, panic protocols, and team-based response.

Realtor & Field Agent Safety Training

Situational awareness for solo showings, open houses, and unfamiliar properties. Covers scheduling safeguards, exit planning, and personal safety tools.

Secure Courier & Transport Services

Chain-of-custody transport for sensitive documents, valuables, and time-critical deliveries, handled by trained, background-checked personnel.

Policy & Compliance Support

Audit-ready workplace safety policy drafted or revised to match current regulations and your actual operations, not a boilerplate template.

About

Twenty years on the job. Zero interest in theory-only training.

Aegis Standard was founded by Marcus Reyes, a retired patrol sergeant and tactical instructor with over two decades of law enforcement and private security experience. He built this company after watching too many organizations run through safety training that looked good on paper and fell apart under real pressure.

Every course is built from what actually happens in the field: the calls that went sideways, the warning signs that got missed, the response that worked because someone had trained for it. Sessions are hands-on, scenario-driven, and built to be remembered, not just completed for a compliance checkbox.

  • 20+ years in law enforcement and private security
  • Certified de-escalation and use-of-force instructor
  • Former tactical response team supervisor
  • Trained staff across healthcare, real estate, retail, and logistics sectors
